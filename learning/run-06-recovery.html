😴 Recovery and sleep

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: Every week

1 · The core concept

Training doesn't make you fitter by itself: it tires you. You get fitter while you rest, when your body repairs and comes back a little stronger. Rest days, sleep and an easier week every 4th week are part of the plan. Without them, the tiredness just piles up.

📉Run: a small dip
A run tires your muscles and tissues for a day or two.
🛌Rest: repair
Sleep and easy days let your body rebuild.
📈Then: stronger
You end up a little fitter than before, ready for the next run.

4 · Step-by-step

1

A run makes you tired first

▶ Show
  • Feeling tired or a bit sore after a run is normal.
  • Muscle soreness that peaks 1–2 days later (called DOMS) is normal too, and fades within about 3 days.
2

Rest days do the repair

▶ Show
  • At least one rest day between runs in phases 1–2.
  • A rest day can include walking, stretching or strength work, but no running.
3

Sleep: your best recovery tool

▶ Show
  • Adults need about 7–9 hours a night.
  • Same bedtime every night, phone outside the bedroom: it's your dashboard habit, and it's also your recovery plan.
4

An easier week every 4th week

▶ Show
  • Weeks 12, 16, 20 and 24 have less running.
  • They feel too easy. That's the point: your body catches up.

Soreness: OK to run easy

  • Dull, spread over a muscle
  • Both legs about the same
  • Gets better as you warm up

Pain: stop and rest

  • Sharp, in one spot, or in a joint
  • Makes you limp or change your stride
  • Gets worse during the run or the next day

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Running every dayMore runs feel like more progress.
✓ FixRest days are training. Keep at least one between runs in phases 1–2. See ✗ Wrong.
✗ Skipping the easier weeks"I feel fine, so I'll run more."
✓ FixDo them anyway: they let you keep building for months.
✗ Short sleep before long runsLate nights on Friday before the Saturday long run.
✓ FixProtect the night before a long run: in bed by 11 pm.
✗ Running through painHoping it goes away.
✓ FixUse the pain rule on the Running page: rest 2 days, then repeat the last easy week.
