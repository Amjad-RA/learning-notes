⏱️ The walk–run method

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: 8 weeks, 3 sessions a week

1 · The core concept

Your heart and lungs get fitter in weeks, but tendons, bones and joints take months. Walk breaks let you run more minutes in total while giving your legs time to adapt. Each week the jogs get a little longer, until you can jog 30 minutes non-stop.

🧱Small blocks
Short jogs with walks in between feel easy, so you keep going.
🦵Joint-friendly
Walk breaks lower the load on knees and ankles while they adapt.
📈Steady steps up
Each week adds a little. If a week feels hard, you repeat it.

3 · Before you start

  • Read tutorial 1 (form) once.
  • A watch or phone app with an interval timer (most free running apps have one).
  • Three fixed days with a rest day between, for example Tue, Thu, Sat.

4 · Step-by-step

1

Weeks 1–2: tiny jogs

▶ Show
  • Warm up with a brisk 5-minute walk.
  • Week 1: 8 × (jog 1 min, walk 2 min). Week 2: 6 × (jog 2 min, walk 2 min).
  • Jog slowly enough to talk. The walks are part of the plan.
2

Weeks 3–4: longer jogs

▶ Show
  • Week 3: 5 × (jog 3 min, walk 2 min). Week 4: 4 × (jog 5 min, walk 2 min).
  • Keep the walks brisk, not strolling.
3

Weeks 5–6: fewer, longer blocks

▶ Show
  • Week 5: 3 × (jog 8 min, walk 2 min). Week 6: 2 × (jog 12 min, walk 2 min).
  • If a jog feels too hard, slow down rather than stopping.
4

Weeks 7–8: non-stop

▶ Show
  • Week 7: jog 20 min without stopping. Week 8: 25–30 min.
  • Done? You have finished phase 1. Check its "Ready when" line on the Running page.
Repeat rule: if you couldn't finish a session, or something still aches the next morning, repeat that week. Repeating is normal and smart, not a failure.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Jogging too fastThe jogs feel hard, so you have to stop early.
✓ FixJog so slowly you could chat. A brisk walk can be almost as fast. That's fine.
✗ Skipping the walksYou feel good and run straight through.
✓ FixDo the plan as written. Your legs adapt more slowly than your lungs.
✗ Running days in a rowTwo sessions back-to-back in week 1.
✓ FixLeave at least one rest day between sessions in phase 1.
✗ Jumping weeksGoing from week 2 straight to week 5.
✓ FixMove up one week at a time, only when the current week felt OK.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Set up the interval timer for this week's session.
  • Session 1 done (note how hard it felt, out of 10).
  • Session 2 done.
  • Session 3 done. Decide: next week or repeat?
