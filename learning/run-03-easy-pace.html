💬 Easy pace and Zone 2

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: Every run, from week 1

1 · The core concept

Most of your running should be easy, around 80%. Easy running builds the heart, lungs and the leg tissue that carries your weight, and you recover quickly from it. Zone 2 is simply a name for this easy effort. You don't need a heart-rate monitor: if you can talk in full sentences, you're there.

💬Talk test
Full sentences = easy. A few words = tempo. Can't talk = hard.
🔟Effort out of 10
RPE: how hard it feels, from 1 (resting) to 10 (all-out). Easy is 3–4.
❤️Heart rate (optional)
Zone 2 is roughly 60–70% of your maximum heart rate. Age formulas are only rough guesses, so trust the talk test first.

  • Read tutorial 2 (walk–run).

4 · Step-by-step

1

Easy: most runs

▶ Show
  • Say your test sentence out loud. You can say it without gasping → easy.
  • Effort 3–4 out of 10. You feel you could keep going for a long time.
  • Too hard? Slow down or walk for a minute. That's part of the plan.
2

Tempo: later, once a week

▶ Show
  • Comfortably hard: only a few words at a time. Effort 6–7.
  • Starts in phase 3 (tutorial 11). Not now.
3

Hard: short bursts only

▶ Show
  • You can't talk. Effort 8–9.
  • Only short intervals in phase 3, with easy jogging between.
4

Your week in effort

▶ Show
  • Phases 1–2: every run is easy.
  • Phase 3: one quality session a week, everything else easy.
  • Easy days easy, so the hard day can be hard.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Every run at medium effortNot easy, not hard: the "grey zone". You're tired all the time and don't improve faster.
✓ FixMake easy runs truly easy. Use the talk test every 10 minutes.
✗ Racing your old timesTrying to beat last week's pace on every run.
✓ FixTrack minutes and how you feel, not pace, in phases 1–2.
✗ Trusting 220 minus ageThe formula can be off by 10 or more beats.
✓ FixUse the talk test first. Heart rate is a helper, not the boss.
✗ Feeling embarrassed to go slowlySo you speed up when people pass.
✓ FixSlow is the method. Most runners' easy runs are slower than they think.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Do the talk test on every jog this week.
  • Write effort out of 10 after each run in your journal.
  • If any run was above 5 out of 10, slow down next time.
  • Optional: note your average heart rate on one easy run.
