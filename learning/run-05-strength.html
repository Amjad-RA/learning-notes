🏋️ Strength for runners

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: 20 min, twice a week

1 · The core concept

Running is thousands of small one-leg landings. Stronger hips, thighs and calves absorb that load, so your knees and ankles don't have to. Two short strength sessions a week are one of the best ways to stay injury-free. You only need your body weight and a chair.

🦵Muscles protect joints
Strong muscles take load off tendons and joints.
🪑Bodyweight first
A chair, a step and the floor. Add weight later.
📈Progressive overload
Make it a little harder over time: more reps first, then a harder version.

2 · Interactive 3D breakdown

Tap a phase or a step card to jump there. Switch to ✗ Wrong on the squat to see knees caving in and heels lifting.

3 · Before you start

  • A sturdy chair or box (seat about knee height).
  • A low, stable step (a stair works). Hold a rail.
  • A mat or carpet for floor work.
  • Strength days on non-running days, for example Wed and Sun.

4 · Step-by-step

1

Box squat: 2–3 × 8–12

▶ Show
  • Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out.
  • Sit back to touch the chair, then stand up. Push the floor away.
  • Knees follow your toes. Heels stay down. Chest up.
2

Glute bridge: 2–3 × 10–15

▶ Show
  • Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat.
  • Squeeze your glutes (bottom muscles) and lift your hips until your body is a straight line from knees to shoulders.
  • Hold 2 seconds at the top, then lower slowly.
3

Calf raise: 2–3 × 12–15

▶ Show
  • Hold a wall. Rise onto the balls of your feet, then lower slowly (count 3).
  • Later: one leg at a time, or on a step edge.
4

Step-up: 2 × 8 each leg

▶ Show
  • Put one foot on a low step and stand up through that leg.
  • Step down slowly. Don't push off with the back foot.
  • Plus 2 core moves: plank 3 × 20–30 s and side plank 2 × 15–20 s each side (knees down is fine).
WeeksHow to progress
1–22 sets of the lower reps. Learn the movement; finish feeling you could do 3–4 more.
3–43 sets. Add 1–2 reps a week until you reach the top of the range.
5+Harder version: one-leg calf raises, a lower chair, a higher step, or hold a backpack with books.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Knees caving inKnees fall toward each other as you stand up.
✓ FixPush your knees out over your little toes. See ✗ Wrong on the squat.
✗ Heels liftingYour weight goes to your toes and the knees take the strain.
✓ FixSit back more and keep your whole foot on the floor.
✗ Rushing the repsBouncing up and down fast.
✓ FixSlow on the way down (2–3 s), steady on the way up.
✗ Strength on the day before a long runTired legs on the important run.
✓ FixStrength the day after a run, or on easy days.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Session 1: squat, bridge, calf raise, step-up, plank, side plank.
  • Session 2: same, on another non-running day.
  • Write sets × reps in your journal.
  • Rate muscle soreness the next day (out of 10).
