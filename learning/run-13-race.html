🏁 Taper and race day

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: The last 2 weeks

1 · The core concept

In the last two weeks you run less, so your legs arrive fresh. The fitness is already built; the taper lets you use it. On race day, the plan is simple: nothing new, start slower than everyone around you, hold a steady effort, and speed up only in the last 2 km if you feel strong.

📉Taper: run about a third less
Keep a few strides so your legs stay sharp.
🧪Nothing new on race day
Same shoes, same breakfast, same kit you've trained in.
🧊Start cool, finish strong
The first 2 km are the slowest.

3 · Before you start

  • A 10 km race entered for late May 2027 (check local race calendars).
  • Long run of 10–11 km done in week 24.
  • Race-day kit tested on long runs.

4 · Step-by-step

1

Taper (weeks 25–26)

▶ Show
  • Week 25: about a third less running. Keep 4 strides on one run.
  • Race week: 2–3 short easy runs. Sleep well; it's still your best recovery.
2

Race morning

▶ Show
  • Breakfast you've tested, about 2–3 hours before.
  • Arrive early, walk and jog 5–10 minutes to warm up, do a few strides.
  • Start near the back of the crowd.
3

Km 0–2: hold back

▶ Show
  • Everyone starts too fast. Let them go.
  • Talk-test pace.
4

Km 3–8: steady

▶ Show
  • Hold the same effort. Drink at water stations if you need to.
  • Check your form when it gets tough: tall, relaxed, quick steps.
5

Km 9–10: go

▶ Show
  • Feeling strong? Speed up gradually.
  • Finish, walk, drink, and write down your time. You did it.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Cramming training in the last weekTrying to get fitter at the last minute.
✓ FixFitness is built. Run less and stay fresh.
✗ New shoes or food on race dayBlisters or an upset stomach.
✓ FixOnly what you've tested on long runs.
✗ Starting fast with the crowdRunning the first km far quicker than planned.
✓ FixStart at the back and hold back for 2 km.
✗ No plan for the hard partKm 7–8 feel tough and you stop.
✓ FixExpect it. Slow down a little; walk 30 seconds if needed, then carry on.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Week 25: cut running by about a third and keep 4 strides.
  • Lay out race kit the night before: shoes, socks, bib, watch.
  • Race: first 2 km slowest, steady middle, faster last 2 km if strong.
  • Write your time and choose the next goal.
