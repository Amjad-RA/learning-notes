5️⃣ Your first 5 km

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: Week 16 test, then any time

1 · The core concept

Running 5 km without stopping is a big milestone, and the secret is pacing. Most first attempts go wrong in the first kilometre: you feel fresh, start too fast, and have to walk later. Start slower than feels necessary, settle in, and only speed up near the end.

🐢First km: slowest
Hold back while you feel fresh.
➡️Km 2–4: steady
Same easy effort. The talk test still works.
🏁Km 5: if you feel good
Then you can speed up a little.

3 · Before you start

  • You can run 30–40 minutes non-stop (end of phase 1 and early phase 2).
  • A measured 5 km route, or a running app that tracks distance.
  • Run it on a day after a rest day.

4 · Step-by-step

1

Km 1: start slower

▶ Show
  • Begin at a pace that feels almost too easy.
  • If you're out of breath after 1 km, you started too fast. Slow down now and you'll still finish.
2

Km 2–4: settle in

▶ Show
  • Keep the same easy effort. On hills, effort stays the same and pace drops.
  • Check your form: tall, relaxed, quick steps.
3

Km 5: finish strong

▶ Show
  • If you feel good, speed up a little for the last 500 m.
  • Walk 5 minutes after you finish, then do your calf stretch.
4

Write it down

▶ Show
  • Time, how it felt (out of 10), and anything that hurt.
  • This is your 5 km baseline. You'll compare against it later.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Starting too fastThe first km feels easy, so you push.
✓ FixMake the first km the slowest. See ✗ Wrong.
✗ Racing itTreating the test like a race and going all out.
✓ FixIt's a test of distance, not speed. Talk-test pace.
✗ Stopping when it gets hardThinking a walk means failure.
✓ FixSlow right down first. If you need to walk, walk 1 minute and carry on.
✗ Testing when tiredAfter a bad night or a hard day.
✓ FixPick a fresh day after a rest day.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Plan your 5 km route or set your app to show distance.
  • Week 16: run the 5 km test at an easy pace.
  • Record time, effort and how you felt.
  • Next day: rest or walk only.
