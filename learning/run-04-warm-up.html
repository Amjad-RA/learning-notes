🔥 Warm-up and cool-down

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: 10 minutes per run

1 · The core concept

Five minutes of brisk walking and a few loose leg movements warm your muscles and get your joints moving before you jog. After the run, walk to bring your breathing down, then stretch gently while your muscles are warm. It's a small habit that makes every run feel better.

🚶Before: move, don't hold
Walking and swinging movements prepare your legs better than long holds.
🦶Ankles and calves
They take a lot of load in running.
🧘After: slow down, then stretch
Gentle 20–30 second holds help you feel looser.

3 · Before you start

  • A wall, fence or tree to hold for balance.
  • Add 10 minutes to each session's plan.

4 · Step-by-step

1

Brisk walk, 5 minutes

▶ Show
  • Walk faster than normal, arms swinging.
  • It is already in every walk–run session from tutorial 2.
2

Leg swings, 10 each leg

▶ Show
  • Hold a wall with one hand and swing the outside leg forward and back.
  • Start small, then let it grow. Stay tall; don't force the height.
3

Ankle circles, 10 each way

▶ Show
  • Lift one foot slightly and draw circles with your toes.
  • Then 10 the other way, and switch feet.
4

Cool-down: walk, then calf stretch

▶ Show
  • After the run, walk 5 minutes until your breathing is calm.
  • Calf stretch: hands on a wall, back leg straight, heel down, lean in gently. Hold 20–30 s each leg, 2 times.
  • Mild pull only, never pain.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Long static stretches before runningHolding stretches for a long time before a run doesn't prepare you to run.
✓ FixKeep the holds for after. Before: walk and swing.
✗ Starting the first jog fastYour body isn't warm yet, so it feels terrible.
✓ FixThe first jog of every session is the slowest.
✗ Stopping dead after the runSitting down straight away while your heart is still pumping hard.
✓ FixWalk 5 minutes first.
✗ Bouncing in stretchesJerky pushes to go further.
✓ FixHold still, breathe slowly, ease in a little more on each breath out.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Add leg swings and ankle circles after your 5-minute walk on all 3 runs.
  • Walk 5 minutes after each run.
  • Calf stretch after each run, both legs, 2 × 30 s.
  • Note any tight spot in your journal.
