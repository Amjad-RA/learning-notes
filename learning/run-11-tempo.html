⚡ Tempo and intervals

Track: RunningIntermediatePractice: 1 session a week, weeks 19–24

1 · The core concept

From week 19, one run a week can be a quality session: a bit of harder running inside an easy run. A tempo run is comfortably hard (effort 6–7, only a few words). Intervals are short hard bursts (effort 8–9) with easy jogging between. Everything else stays easy.

🔥Warm-up first
10 minutes of easy jogging before the hard part.
📊Effort, not pace
Use the talk test and effort out of 10.
1️⃣Only once a week
One quality session. The other runs stay easy.

4 · Step-by-step

1

Warm up: 10 min easy + 2–3 strides

▶ Show
  • Always start easy. Your legs need time before hard running.
2

Tempo (weeks 19–20)

▶ Show
  • Start with 2 × 8 min at effort 6–7, with 2 min easy jogging between.
  • Build towards 20 min continuous over the following weeks.
3

Intervals (weeks 21–23)

▶ Show
  • Start with 4 × 2 min at effort 8–9, with 2 min easy jog between.
  • Build to 6 × 2 min. The last one should feel like the first: if not, you went too fast.
4

Cool down: 10 min easy

▶ Show
  • Then walk and stretch (tutorial 4).
  • Week 24 and race weeks: just a few strides, no hard session.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ Too hardIntervals at sprint effort, tempo at race effort.
✓ FixTempo 6–7, intervals 8–9. You finish feeling you could do one more.
✗ Skipping the warm-upStraight into the hard part.
✓ Fix10 minutes easy first, every time.
✗ Two hard days a weekLong run fast plus intervals.
✓ FixOnly one quality session. The long run stays easy.
✗ Doing it when sorePushing on tired legs.
✓ FixSwap it for an easy run and try again next week.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Pick your quality day (away from the long run).
  • Warm up 10 min, then this week's session from the table.
  • Cool down 10 min, then stretch.
  • Write down effort for each block and how the last one felt.
