🍽️ Fuel and weight loss

Track: RunningBeginnerPractice: Every day

1 · The core concept

You lose weight when you take in a little less energy (food) than you use. Running raises what you use, but it also raises your hunger. The aim is a gentle gap: you lose weight slowly (about 0.5 kg a week or less) and still have energy to train and recover. A big gap leaves you tired, hungry and more likely to get injured.

⚖️A gentle gap
Slow and steady loss keeps your energy for running.
🍗Protein at every meal
It helps your muscles recover and keeps you full.
🍌Carbs around runs
Fuel for longer runs; don't cut them on long-run days.

3 · Before you start

  • A kitchen scale is optional; a bathroom scale for weekly weight.
  • Your weight on the same morning each week (after the toilet, before eating).
  • If you have a health condition or take medicine, ask a doctor or dietitian for a personal plan.

4 · Step-by-step

1

Balance: weight stays the same

▶ Show
  • If food in = energy out, your weight stays stable.
  • Watch your weekly average weight, not daily ups and downs.
2

Gentle gap: slow loss

▶ Show
  • Eat a little less than you use: smaller portions, fewer sugary drinks and snacks.
  • Aim for about 0.5 kg a week or less. That's steady and safe.
3

Too big a gap: avoid

▶ Show
  • Skipping meals or crash diets → tired runs, poor sleep, cravings, injuries.
  • If runs feel flat for a week, eat a bit more.
4

Fuel the run

▶ Show
  • 2–3 hours before: a light meal with carbs (toast, oats, banana, rice).
  • After: protein plus carbs within a couple of hours (eggs, dal and rice, curd, chicken).
  • Drink water through the day; during runs over about an hour, too.

5 · Common mistakes and fixes

✗ "I ran, so I can eat anything"Running burns less than most people think.
✓ FixKeep normal portions; plan one treat rather than adding many.
✗ Crash dietingBig cuts to lose weight fast.
✓ FixGentle gap: about 0.5 kg a week or less.
✗ No carbs before long runsRunning out of energy halfway.
✓ FixA carb-based light meal 2–3 hours before.
✗ Weighing daily and panickingWater and food change the scale day to day.
✓ FixWeigh once a week, same morning, and watch the trend.

6 · Practice drill: this week

  • Weigh yourself once this week (same morning routine) and write it down.
  • Add a protein food to each meal.
  • Swap one sugary drink or snack a day for water or fruit.
  • Before your long run: light carb meal 2–3 h before.
