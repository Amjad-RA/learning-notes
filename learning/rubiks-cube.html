1 · Learn the moves first

Hold the cube with one face pointing at you. Each letter names a face. Turn it the way a clock turns, as if you are looking straight at that face.

RRight
LLeft
UUp (top)
DDown
FFront
BBack

Read it like this

  • R = right face, clockwise
  • R' ("R prime") = right face, anticlockwise
  • R2 = right face, turned twice
  • Old notes: R1 = R'

Common mistakes

  • Turning the whole cube instead of one face
  • Looking at the front while turning L, D or B: look from that face's own side
Tap a move to see it. White is on top and green is at the front.

2 · The 7 steps

Tick "I can do this step" once you can do it without looking. Your progress shows on the Learning page.

Every cube shows the step's situation, then solves it when you press ▶. Use Next move to go one turn at a time and copy each turn on your own cube.
1

White cross ⬜➕

Goal: a white plus on top, and each edge's side colour matches the centre below it.

  1. Hold white on top. Find a white edge piece (2 colours).
  2. If it is in the bottom layer, turn D until it sits under its matching centre, then turn that face twice: F2
  3. If it is on top but white faces outwards (flipped): F U' R U
  4. No fixed algorithm here: work it out slowly. That's normal.
2

White corners

Goal: the whole white face is done, and the first layer's sides match their centres.

  1. Turn the cube over: white is now at the bottom and stays there for the rest of the solve.
  2. Find a corner with white in the top layer. Turn U until it sits right above its place, at the front right.
  3. Repeat R U R' U' until the corner drops in correctly (1, 3 or 5 times).
3

Middle layer: top to left, top to right

Goal: the first two layers are finished.

  1. Find a top edge with no yellow. Turn U until its front colour matches the front centre (an upside-down T).
  2. Top to left: if its top colour matches the left centre, do U' L' U L U F U' F'
  3. Top to right: if it matches the right centre, do U R U' R' U' F' U F
  4. Edge stuck in the wrong slot? Do either one once to pop it out, then put it in again.
4

Make the yellow cross 🟨➕

Goal: a yellow plus on top (the corners don't matter yet).

Algorithm: F U R U' R' F'

  1. Dot (only the centre is yellow): do it once from any side.
  2. Line: do it once from any side. You will get an L.
  3. L shape: hold the L so its two yellow edges are at the back and on the left. Do it once, and the cross is done.
5

Finish the yellow face

Goal: the whole top is yellow.

Algorithm: R U R' U R U2 R' (repeat until the top is yellow, at most 3 times)

  1. Count the corners with yellow on top, then hold the cube like this before each go:
  2. No yellow corner: the front-left corner has yellow facing left.
  3. One yellow corner: put it at the front left.
  4. Two yellow corners: the front-left corner has yellow facing the front.
6

Corners: the U shape on every side

Goal: each side's two top corners are the same colour (a "U shape", or "headlights").

Algorithm: R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2

  1. Look for a side whose two top corners match: that is a U shape.
  2. Hold that side at the back, then do the algorithm.
  3. No U shape anywhere? Do it once from any side, then look again.
  4. Once all four sides have a U shape, turn U until the corners match their side centres.
7

Final step: solve the cube 🎉

Goal: solved!

Algorithm: R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R'

  1. Find the side that is fully solved (its whole top row matches) and hold it at the back.
  2. Do the algorithm. If the cube isn't solved yet, do it once more, holding the solved side at the back again.
  3. No solved side? Do it once from any side. One side will be solved; then go back to point 1.
In your old notes this is written R2 U R U R1 U1 R1 U1 R1 U R1. There, R1 and U1 mean R' and U'.

🔤

Moves without looking

Read an algorithm aloud and do it with your eyes closed.

5 min · until no move needs thought

🔁

Algorithm reps

Do each algorithm 20 times in a row, slowly at first. A solved cube comes back after 6 × R U R' U', so you can check yourself.

10 min · one algorithm a day

👆

Finger tricks

Turn U with a right-index flick and R with your wrist. Don't move your grip between moves.

Time 10 × R U R' U' and beat yesterday's time

➕

Cross only

Scramble the cube and solve only the white cross. Plan all 4 edges before your first turn.

10 crosses · aim for 8 moves or fewer

🐢

Slow, no stopping

Solve at half speed without pausing. While you turn, look for the next piece. This is called "look-ahead".

3 slow solves a day

⏱️

5 timed solves

Use the timer below. Your "average of 5" leaves out the best and the worst time. Use it to track your progress.

Once a day · write your best average in your journal

Your steps 4–7 are the "4-look last layer" that many speedcubers start with. Once you are steady under 1:30, the next upgrade is learning the 7 yellow-corner cases, so step 5 always takes one go instead of up to 3. Not before: speed comes from smooth turning first.
